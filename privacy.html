Privacy Policy

Live at The Oak (web & iOS app) — last updated August 2026

The short version

No accounts. No tracking. No analytics. No ads. The only thing the game ever sends anywhere is a high score, and only when you press "Post score".

What is collected

If you choose to post a score to the leaderboard, we store:

  • the three letters you enter as initials
  • your score, accuracy and best combo for that song

That's the entire record. It is not linked to your identity, your device, or anything else, and it is displayed publicly on the in-game leaderboard. Initials are chosen by you and need not identify you.

What stays on your device

Your saved initials and audio calibration setting are stored locally on your device and never transmitted.

What is not collected

No names, emails, contacts, location, identifiers, usage analytics, advertising data, or anything else. The iOS app works fully offline; the leaderboard is its only network feature.

Removal

Want a posted score removed from the leaderboard? Email the address below with the song, initials and score, and it will be deleted.
